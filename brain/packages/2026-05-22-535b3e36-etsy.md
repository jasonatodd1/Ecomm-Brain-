## Description (Etsy plaintext — paste verbatim)

```
An A5 monthly calendar printable for planner people tired of daily and weekly grids they never use — month-at-a-glance pages plus three note styles in one undated PDF.

WHY THIS ONE

Most A5 planner inserts force daily or weekly structure, and the ones that don't usually make you buy the monthly calendar, plain notes, lined notes, and dot-grid pages as four separate listings. This is the bundle that solves it — both Sunday-start and Monday-start monthly calendars, plus all three note styles, in one minimalist PDF. Undated means it never expires, and the warm-neutral Scandinavian aesthetic sits quietly in your binder instead of fighting with florals and boho patterns.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

- Undated monthly calendar, Sunday start (12 pages)
- Undated monthly calendar, Monday start (12 pages)
- Plain note page (1 page)
- Lined note page (1 page)
- Dot-grid note page (1 page)
- Cover/dashboard page (1 page)
- Printing & trim guide (1 page)

HOW IT WORKS

1. Purchase and download the PDF instantly after checkout — no waiting, no shipping.
2. Print at home on A5 paper, or print on US Letter and trim to A5 using the included guide.
3. Hole-punch for any standard A5 6-ring binder (Filofax, Kikki-K, Van der Spek, generic).
4. Use the Sunday-start or Monday-start calendar — both are included, pick what fits your week.
5. Reprint as many copies of the note pages as you need; the file is yours for life.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q. Is this dated for a specific year?
A. No — every calendar page is undated. Write in the month and year yourself so you can start any month and reuse the file year after year.

Q. Will the notes pages be enough, or do I need to buy separate paper?
A. The PDF includes plain, lined, and dot-grid templates — print as many copies of each as you want. You won't need to source separate note inserts.

Q. Is the design original?
A. Yes, every page is designed in-house by HillwardStudio. The minimalist Scandinavian aesthetic — hairline rules, small-caps headers, warm-neutral tone — is built specifically for this listing.

Q. What software do I need to open it?
A. Any free PDF reader (Adobe Acrobat Reader, Preview on Mac, or your browser) will open and print the file. No special software or subscription required.

Q. Can I print this at home?
A. Yes. Print directly on A5 paper, or print on US Letter at 100% scale and trim to A5 using the included guide. Inner margins are sized for standard A5 6-ring binder hole punches.

Q. Will it fit my Filofax / Kikki-K / generic A5 binder?
A. Yes — the pages are true A5 (148 × 210 mm) with extra inner margin to accommodate 6-ring hole punching without cutting into the design.

Q. Is anything shipped?
A. No, this is a digital download only. Your files are available immediately after purchase in your Etsy account under Purchases & Reviews.

—

Thanks for stopping by HillwardStudio. Every piece in the shop is designed with the same intention — quiet, considered, and built to earn its place rather than disappear into a download folder.
```
