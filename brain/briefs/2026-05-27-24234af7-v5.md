## Audience Persona

**Persona:** The single-page meal planner — a busy parent or working adult who plans dinner on Sunday morning at the kitchen table, wants the week's meals and the grocery list visible at the same time, and is tired of either toggling between spreadsheet tabs or printing two sheets that get separated on the way to the store.

**Primary search intent:** Searching 'meal planner printable with grocery list' because they want a one-page weekly system they can stick on the fridge and take to the store, not a multi-page bundle or a Google Sheets setup.

**Decision factors:**
- meal plan and grocery list on a single page
- grocery list grouped by store aisle, not blank lines
- both Sunday-start and Monday-start included
- instant PDF — no app or login required
- under $5 — low risk from a new shop
