*Mitigation:* Price at $4.99 (below both direct comps), lead with the heirloom-bunny differentiation in title + hero image, and rely on long-tail SEO ('vintage bunny nursery', 'heirloom nursery print', 'grandmillennial nursery') where incumbents under-target.

### HIGH: Aesthetic execution risk: 'vintage storybook watercolor' is a fine line — done poorly it reads AI-generated or generic; done well it reads heirloom. Failure here kills conversion regardless of SEO.
*Mitigation:* Design agent must reference Beatrix-Potter-era plates explicitly, include visible ink linework under washes, aged paper texture, and avoid the smooth-gradient look that signals AI. Hero image must showcase fine detail.

### MEDIUM: IP perception — buyers may suspect AI generation or public-domain repurposing, both of which suppress favorites and risk Etsy enforcement.
*Mitigation:* Address originality explicitly in FAQ #1. Hero and lifestyle_detail images must show enough texture and linework that the buyer reads it as a real watercolor.

### MEDIUM: Google Trends signal is +319% velocity off a small base (~30 weekly searches) — could be noise rather than durable demand.
*Mitigation:* Lean SEO on durable evergreen terms ('nursery wall art printable', 'cottagecore nursery decor') rather than trend-chasing language; treat the trend signal as a tailwind, not the basis for the listing.

### MEDIUM: Median favorers across the 100-listing sample is only 8, meaning most listings in this broad category never gain traction. Execution and SEO matter more than the niche signal.
