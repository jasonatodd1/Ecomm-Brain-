## Audience Persona

**Persona:** The pragmatic weeknight household manager — primary parent or partner in a 2-5 person home who plans meals on Sunday, shops once or twice a week, and is tired of meal planner PDFs that split meals and the shopping list across separate pages they have to print, flip, or rewrite.

**Primary search intent:** Searching 'meal planner printable' or 'weekly meal planner with grocery list' because they want one cohesive printable they can stick on the fridge Sunday and bring to the store Wednesday.

**Decision factors:**
- meal plan and grocery list on the same page
- aisle-grouped grocery sheet included
- undated so it never expires
- clean neutral aesthetic that fits a kitchen
- instant download in both Letter and A4

## Differentiation Thesis (product-gap axis)

**Positioning:** The one-page weekly meal + grocery spread for busy households — everything you need for the week visible at once, no flipping between planner and shopping list.

**One-line claim:** A weekly meal planner printable where your full week of meals and the grocery list live on a single page — designed to be torn off, taken to the store, and done.

**Our differentiation:** Most incumbents (e.g. 4324838798) ship the meal planner and grocery list as SEPARATE pages forcing buyers to flip between them mid-shop, and the pre-categorised grocery list options are rigid. Our v1 ships a single-spread weekly layout where each day's meals and the consolidated grocery list live on ONE printable page — eliminating page-flipping during shopping — plus a second 'aisle-grouped' grocery sheet (produce / proteins / pantry / dairy / freezer) that addresses the 'incomplete bundle scope' pain by including the categorisation buyers want without inflating page count.
