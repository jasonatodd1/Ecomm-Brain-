*Mitigation:* Lead the listing copy and whats_included image with the bundle's scope — both week-starts, three note styles, cover, and printing guide — and frame the leanness as intentional minimalism rather than incomplete. FAQ explicitly addresses 'will this be enough'.

### LOW: Common-paper-style infringement concerns are negligible, but design originality could be questioned given how simple minimalist grids are.
*Mitigation:* FAQ #3 affirms HillwardStudio originates the design in-house; keep file metadata clean; archive source files (Figma/Affinity) with timestamps in case of dispute.

### LOW: If launch slips past Q1 2026, undated positioning becomes the only viable angle (dated competitors flood the category in January).
*Mitigation:* Undated is already the chosen format, so timing risk is structurally hedged. v2 dated 2026/2027 variant can be added later as a same-listing variation or separate listing.
